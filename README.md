# My Favorite Movie: WALL-E

**WALL-E** is an animated science-fiction movie about a curious robot who discovers hope on an abandoned Earth.

## Story Overview

WALL-E cleans up the trash left behind by humans. His routine changes when EVE arrives to search for signs of life. Their adventure explores *friendship*, **courage**, and **environmental responsibility**.

> Small actions can lead to meaningful change.
>
> — My takeaway from the movie

## Main Characters

| Character | Role | Personality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WALL-E | A robot who cleans Earth | Curious and caring |
| EVE | A robot searching for plant life | Brave and determined |
| Captain McCrea | Captain of the Axiom | Responsible and willing to learn |

## Why I Like the Movie

- **Visual storytelling:** The characters express emotions through movement and gestures.
- **Friendship:** WALL-E and EVE care for each other through their actions.
- **Environmental message:** The story encourages people to care for Earth.
- **Hope:** The characters show that positive change is possible.

### Lessons I Learned

1. Protect the environment through everyday choices.
2. Stay curious and appreciate simple things.
3. Help others, even when it takes courage.

## Connecting the Movie to Programming

WALL-E's repeated cleanup work can be represented using a loop:

```text
WHILE there is litter nearby
    pick up an item
    sort it for proper disposal
END WHILE
```

The loop shows how small, repeated steps can accomplish a large task.
